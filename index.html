CogRush

Maze your lane. Send robots into theirs.
Last one with lives wins.

How to play

Build: tap a cell, pick a tower. Every tower is also a wall: robots take the shortest open path, so make it long. Drag across cells to build a whole row.

Don't seal the lane: if no path is left, robots go berserk and blast through (Breach mode), your income freezes and leaks cost double.

Send: load robots, then Release. Every robot you send raises your income for the rest of the match. The bot sees your wave 4 s before it lands, and you see theirs.

Counter: Kinetic beats Light, Explosive beats Heavy, Energy beats Shielded. Factories unlock robots; you only get 4 slots.

Keys: press H in a match for the full list.

How to play

Lives ❤: every robot reaching your exit costs 1 (2 while your lane is sealed). 0 lives = you lose.

Income ▲: paid every 5 s (green bar under the stats). Every 10 gold of robots you send adds +1 for the rest of the match. Income freezes while your lane is sealed.

Build: tap a cell and pick a tower, or drag across cells to build a row of the tower picked in the Build tab. Robots take the shortest open path, so make it long.

Counter: Kinetic beats Light, Explosive beats Heavy, Energy beats Shielded.
